What we believe

Built like it matters.

Three principles behind everything with our name on it.

I.

Fewer, better.

We keep a short list of products and give each one our full attention. If it isn’t worth doing well, it isn’t worth doing.

II.

Respect is a feature.

No dark patterns. No shouting. No treating you like a metric. Software should earn its place on your phone — and keep earning it.

III.

Built in Savannah.

We’re an independent company in Savannah, Georgia — not a pitch deck with a logo. What we ship has our name on it, and we stand behind it.
