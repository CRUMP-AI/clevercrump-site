Websites

Websites for small businesses.

We build websites for the bakery, the salon, the shop down the street — businesses that need to be found, believed, and contacted. No jargon, no maze of options. You tell us what the site has to do, we build it, and it goes live.

Prices

The price is on the page because small business owners hate “contact for pricing.” Fifty percent up front, fifty percent at launch. Hosting and the domain are separate, or rolled into the care plan — your call.

Starter — $1,500

One to five pages: home, about, services, contact. For a business that needs to be found and believed.

Business — $3,500 to $5,000

Five to ten pages, contact forms, Google setup, and basic search help. Our main offer.

Store or custom — from $7,500

Online ordering, bookings, or anything with real moving parts. Quoted after a look, never flat-rate blind.

Keep it running

A website needs looking after once it is live. The care plan is $99 a month — updates, backups, security, and small edits when you need them. One-off work outside a plan is $75 an hour.
